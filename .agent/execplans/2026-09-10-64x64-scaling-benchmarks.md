# 64x64 scaling benchmarks: Benes and multiport MMI under LiDAR, baseline, contribution 1 and contribution 2

## Purpose

The paper's evaluation stops at 32 inputs. The owner wants one size step beyond that, so that the scaling argument has a data point where the loss-driven routers run into their limits and the pre-placed crossing structures (contribution 2) still solve the circuit. After this plan, the repository has two new benchmarks, `benes_64x64` and `multiportmmi_64x64`, each routable with the same one-flag commands as the smaller sizes, and a recorded result for four configurations per benchmark: the LiDAR reference router, our engine in its baseline `lidar-pure` mode, contribution 1 (`--crossing-mode lidar-guided`) and contribution 2 (`--preplaced-crossing-grids true`), all with a one-hour limit. The owner's expectation, to be confirmed or refuted by the runs: the two loss-driven routers do not finish within the hour, contribution 2 does.

A "benchmark" here is a Python module in `benchmarks/` that builds the circuit (a gdsfactory schematic: components, their placement, and the nets between their ports) and declares the stable routing flags of its baseline. A "planned crossing" is a crossing the topology analysis derives from the port orders before routing; "verification-clean" means the photonic verification JSON in `build/verification/` reports zero errors.

## Milestone 3: Benes in LiDAR's format (done 2026-09-11, owner: "we need these numbers")

LiDAR has no Benes benchmark in any of its checkouts or upstream (the LiDAR 2.0 paper evaluates Benes 16x16 / 32x32 built from MZI switches after Qiao et al. 2017, but neither those benchmarks nor the LiDAR 2.0 code are public). The bridge is two scripts:

Insertion losses per component as in LiDAR's own generators (coupler 2.0, MMI 0.3, heater 0.1 dB), placement halo 10 um, die = layout bounding box + 100 um. The gdsfactory components `mmi2x2`, `straight_heater_metal`, `grating_coupler_te` have bit-identical bounding boxes and ports in LiDAR's gdsfactory 8.26 and our 9.43 (checked), so lower-left anchoring reproduces our placement exactly.

## multiportmmi_128x128 with contribution 2: root cause and fix (2026-09-16 morning)

- The 99 `search-failure-blocker` lines of the first diagnosis belonged to earlier, successful searches (they are printed whenever a landing in the target ring is blocked); the failing net n_1643 never ran a search at all: `native_negotiated_search net=1134 kind=plain elapsed_s=0.000 expanded=0 ... error="No route found (expanded_states=0, window_attempts=5, used_full_grid_fallback=true, last_window_area_cells=121837114)"`.
- Cause: `AStarConfig::max_dense_states` (per-attempt cap on dense search states, 8 per window cell) was a fixed 20 000 000, not configurable from Python, and `DenseSearchStorage::new` returned `None` silently above it. The heater-to-MMI nets of the 128x128 mesh (940 x 2400 cells) get routing windows of 2.7-3.5 M cells = 21.9-28.2 M states, so all five windows and the full-grid fallback (121.8 M cells, 975 M states) failed with zero expansions. The 64x64 mesh's longest windows stay below 20 M states, which is why it never showed.
- Fix (`src/astar.rs`, `src/py_router.rs`): default raised to 100 000 000 states (about 1.7 GB eagerly allocated at the largest allowed window: f64 g-cost, u32 generation, u32 parent, u16 primitive, closed bit), `PHOTONIC_ROUTER_MAX_DENSE_STATES` overrides it (`configured_max_dense_states`, unit test), the cap exit now counts into `dense_grid_build_failures` and reports `search-failure kind=dense_storage_cap window=... states=... max_dense_states=...` under `PHOTONIC_ROUTER_SEARCH_FAILURE_DIAG`. Also new under that flag: `kind=endpoint_blocked` / `kind=dense_grid_not_built` (JPS4 entry), `kind=no_routing_bounds` / `kind=budget_exhausted_before_window` / `kind=windows_exhausted_no_fallback` (window wrapper), and `PHOTONIC_ROUTER_SEARCH_FAILURE_MAP=1` prints an ASCII picture of the obstacle map around a failed search. Trace: `native_negotiated_search ... kind=plain` lines carry `error=`.
- Not resolved by the fix: the full-grid fallback at 128x128 (975 M states) still exceeds the cap by design; nets whose windows all fail legitimately now report `dense_storage_cap` for the fallback instead of searching the whole 122 M-cell grid (42 such reports in the first 10 minutes of the rerun -- they are the crossing-tile-adjacent nets that the negotiated engine then handles).
