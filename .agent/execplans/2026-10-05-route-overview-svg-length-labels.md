# Route overview SVG with per-waveguide length labels and a legend

This ExecPlan is a living document. The sections `Progress`, `Surprises & Discoveries`, `Decision Log`, and `Outcomes & Retrospective` must be kept up to date as work proceeds. This document must be maintained in accordance with `.agent/PLANS.md`.

Status: **2026-10-05 -- COMPLETE (writer, flow integration, per-route labels) plus Milestone 0, the bookkeeping by physical edge the owner asked for after the path-length question; records in Progress and Outcomes.**


## Purpose / Big Picture

The route SVGs the flow writes under `--debug-svgs` show one route per file on the routing grid, without its length, and no picture shows all routed waveguides together with the numbers the owner compares layouts by. After this plan every run with debug SVGs enabled also writes one overview SVG, `build/routes/<benchmark>_overview.svg`, that draws every routed waveguide's centerline in micrometres with a label carrying its length (the routed length, plus the meander length the path-length matching inserted on that waveguide, if any), and a legend with the benchmark name, the number of routes, the total routed length without meanders, the total meander length, the total with meanders, and the chip dimensions (the routable die area and the layout's own bounding box). Each per-route SVG also gets a text line with the route's name and routed length. To see it: `python -m photonic_router route heater_s_mod --debug-svgs 1` prints the route SVG count and the overview path; opening `build/routes/heater_s_mod_overview.svg` shows the 81 waveguides with their lengths and the legend whose meander total equals the SUMMARY line's `meander_inserted_um`.

## Surprises & Discoveries

- 2026-10-05: while preparing the labels, the owner asked whether the path-length matching is realized in the final GDS. It is, within 0.5 um on every cascade variant (state file, 2026-10-05 late; a first version of the check wrongly reported millimetre imbalances and was corrected). But the meander report books a meander on the requiring edge while the geometry sits on a delay-insertion candidate upstream (through heaters, or onto all inputs of an MMI whose outputs all require the same extra), so a label of "routed + inserted" per net is not that net's realized length (8 of 52 meandered nets on `heater_s_mod` differ by up to 3,012 um). Consequence for this plan: the per-route label must use the realized placement -- either measured from the GDS polygons or derived from the planner's candidate placement (`selected_box` and `edge_keys` of the planned candidate) -- not the report's booking; the legend totals (routed sum, meander sum, total) are unaffected.


## Decision Log

- 2026-09-28 (owner): per-waveguide length labels in the route SVGs are a later item; 2026-10-05 (owner): do them now, and put the total length of all routes, the length without meanders, the meander length and the chip dimensions into the SVG as a legend.
- 2026-10-05 (lead): the overview is drawn from the flow's own records, not from the GDS: `RoutedNetRecord.corrected_centerline_um` and `total_length_um` (the routed centerline before meanders) and the meander insertion report's `inserted_extra_length_um` per edge (matched by net name). The meander geometry itself is not redrawn; its length is in the label. Reason: per-net lengths cannot be recovered from the GDS (routed waveguides are polygons in the top cell), while the records are exact and already computed.
- 2026-10-05 (lead): the overview is a debug SVG like the others (written only when `--debug-svgs` is on, with any selector; cleaned up by the same prefix glob), so a default run still writes no SVG (`AGENTS.md`).
- 2026-10-05 (lead): chip dimensions in the legend are the routable die area (`RustRouteDebugArtifacts.routable_bbox_um`: the explicit die bbox, or the component bbox plus the chip margins) as width x height, and the routed layout's own bounding box as a second line.


## Outcomes & Retrospective

Every run with debug SVGs now has one picture of all routed waveguides with their realized lengths and a legend (routes, routed length, meander length, total, die area, layout box), and every per-route SVG names its route and routed length. On the way the owner's question whether the matching is realized in the GDS was answered with a measurement (yes, within 0.5 um) after a wrong first answer from a check that dropped the single-input nodes; the real gap was bookkeeping: the report booked meanders on requiring edges while drawing them on candidates upstream. That bookkeeping now exists per physical edge, the diagnostics and the acceptance check use it, and a GDS test pins it. Lesson: a check that walks a graph must walk every node, and a number read from a report must say which object it describes.


## Context and Orientation

`python/photonic_router/flow.py::route_benchmark` runs the optical stage (`routing_flow_optical.run_photonic_routing_stage`, giving `OpticalRoutingStageResult` with `debug_artifacts: RustRouteDebugArtifacts` -- `routed_net_records`, `route_svgs`, `routable_bbox_um` -- and `route_result` with `meander_insertion_report_info`), then `routing_flow_plm.attach_and_report_path_length_matching` (attaches the path-length analysis and the meander report to `routed_layout.info` and fills `stats.meander_inserted_um`), then the electrical step, then `routing_flow_reporting.report_and_open_debug_svgs`. Per-route SVGs are written during dispatch by `translation/routing/dispatch.py::_export_route_svg` (the Rust router's `export_debug_svg`, grid coordinates, plus an overlay of port-keepout cells appended before `</svg>`) into `build/routes/<prefix>_<net>.svg`; `routing_flow_reporting.cleanup_debug_artifacts` removes `build/routes/<prefix>_*.svg` before a run. A `RoutedNetRecord` holds `net_name`, `total_length_um` (the corrected centerline's length, before meanders), `corrected_centerline_um` (points in um) and `base_total_length_um`. The meander report is a dict with `results`, each `{"edge": {"net_name": ..., "source": ..., "target": ...}, "inserted_extra_length_um": ..., "status": ...}` and `total_inserted_extra_length_um`.


## Milestone 1: the writer

`translation/route_overview_svg.py`: `write_routes_overview_svg(path, *, benchmark_name, records, meander_inserted_um_by_net, routable_bbox_um, layout_bbox_um) -> RoutesOverviewSummary` (a frozen dataclass with the route count, the routed, meander and total lengths and the two bboxes, so the caller can print and test the same numbers the legend shows). Geometry: the drawing area is the union of the two bboxes and every centerline, mapped to a 2000 px wide canvas with a margin, y flipped; each route a polyline (stroke 1 px, one color); each label `<text>` at the polyline's middle vertex with `"<length> um"` or `"<length> um (+<meander> um meander)"`, small font, a light halo for legibility; the die bbox a dashed rectangle, the layout bbox a dotted one; the legend a box in the top-left corner with the six lines. Records without a centerline are listed in the legend as "not drawn". Unit test on three synthetic records (an L, a straight, one with a meander) asserting the summary numbers and that the SVG text contains each label and every legend line.
