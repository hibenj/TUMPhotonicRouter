## Purpose / Big Picture

The six cascade benchmarks (`benchmarks/cascade_{8,12,16}_{folded,diagonal}.py`, built by `benchmarks/cascade.py`) place the cascade MZIs in columns 1,000 um apart. The fold needs that pitch because every column holds two stacked MZIs with a loop-back route between them; the diagonal inherited it on the owner's decision of 2026-09-28 ("the diagonal keeps the folded layout's 1000 um column spacing; a compact diagonal may follow"), although one cascade MZI is only about 586 um long (first MMI 10 um before the column x, heater from +124 to +476, second MMI to +575.5). The 16-mode diagonal is 17.2 mm long against the 12 x 3.25 mm AMF die named in the requirements. After this plan there is a third layout, "compact", a diagonal whose column pitch is the smallest value at which all three sizes route clean with the stable configuration (optical: every net routed, 0 verifier errors; path-length matching: 0 groups over tolerance; electrical: every pad wire routed, verification clean), found by a measured sweep, with three new benchmarks `cascade_{8,12,16}_compact`. To see it: `python -m photonic_router route cascade_16_compact` ends with `SUMMARY ... route_failures=... verifier_errors=0 ... plm_over_tolerance=0 ... electrical_ok=yes`, and the layout is shorter than the diagonal by (N-4) times the pitch reduction.

## Surprises & Discoveries

- 2026-10-05 (first sweep, before the straight-drop phase): clean down to 610 um for 8 modes (at 600 the mode net from `mmi_b_2.o4` into MZI 3's lower input finds no route: MZI 2 and MZI 3 share a row, and the gap between MZI 2's second MMI and MZI 3's first MMI, the pitch minus 585.5 um, is too small for the 90-degree approach), but only to 670 um for 12 modes and 910 um for 16 modes, both with `electrical_ok=no` and zero lost wires: one `metal_overlaps_raw_obstacle` error on the common bus. Traced on the 16-mode case at 900 um: the cascade MZIs of a diagonal stand alone in their columns and rows, so neither the column-trunk phase (groups of two or more heaters per column) nor the local-trunk phase (same-row pairs) serves them, and the greedy search joins each to the nearest tree cell; at 1,000 um the stripe (900 um down) is nearer than the previous heater's trunk (1,000 um left) and the branch drops straight; at 900 um the neighbour's trunk is nearer (880 um) and the branch runs along the heater's own row, where its port adapter covers the via squares of the port cluster outside the allowed contact. A straight drop three columns outward is legal there (`straight_drop_to_bus`, the single-terminal column trunk), but it was only ever tried after a pad-side swap.
- 2026-10-05: the trunk column's own x-cell was free of every blocked cell at 900 um; the straight drop's legality comes from the raw-box band check, not from the grid. The greedy search uses the dilated grid only, which is why its straight branch at 1,000 um was accepted although the same band would have been refused at k = 0 -- the trunk hugging the port cluster lies inside the allowed contact.


## Decision Log

- 2026-09-28 (owner): the diagonal keeps the fold's 1,000 um column pitch; a compact diagonal may follow.
- 2026-10-05 (lead): "compact" means the diagonal with the smallest column pitch at which the 8-, 12- and 16-mode cascades all route clean under the stable configuration, measured by a sweep in 50 um steps from 950 um down (then refined in 10 um steps around the first failure), one pitch for all three sizes so the benchmarks stay comparable. Only the pitch between cascade columns changes: column 0 (x 1,340), the base of column 1 (x 2,200), the lane geometry and the tomography gap stay, so the compact layout differs from the diagonal only in the x of columns 2 and beyond and everything after them. If the owner wants a die-fit target instead (12 mm for the 16-mode), the pitch is one constant.
- 2026-10-05 (lead): the common bus gets a phase 2b, `common_bus_router._route_lone_straight_drops`: every heater left after the column trunks and the local trunks drops straight to the stripe from whichever terminal has a legal column (`straight_drop_to_bus`, same legality rules as the column trunks; the shorter legal drop wins, ties by side key then id); only heaters with no legal drop reach the greedy search. General, not a cascade rule: it is the single-heater case of the column trunk, and the greedy search's sideways join to a neighbour's branch was already the thing the straight drop after a pad-side swap existed to avoid. The four suite cases are byte-identical under it (none has a lone heater the earlier phases leave over); the six existing cascades stay clean.
- 2026-10-05 (lead): the pinned pitch is 620 um, not the measured floor of 610: at 610 the path-length matching needs 2.8 mm more meander on the 16-mode and 1.4 mm more on the 12-mode cascade than at 620 (the last 10 um of room is paid for in matching length), and 610 sits directly on the optical cliff at 600, where any change in net order or search budget would tip it. 620 keeps the 650 um meander lengths with 20 um of margin. The owner can set the constant to 610 if the last 10 um matter more than the robustness.
- 2026-10-05 (lead): the pitch is a keyword parameter of the builder (`column_pitch_um`, default the fold's 1,000), and the compact modules carry the pinned value as a module constant `COLUMN_PITCH_UM` read at build time, so the sweep can set it per run without touching the file.


## Outcomes & Retrospective

Six new benchmarks: `cascade_{8,12,16}_compact`, the diagonal at a 620 um column pitch (from 1,000), and `cascade_{8,12,16}_folded_compact`, the fold at 700 um (16 modes 8.72 mm instead of 10.22; its limit is the room the path-length matching needs for its meanders at 650 um, not the optics). The diagonal ones first, all clean under the stable configuration: 16 modes 12.04 mm long instead of 16.22, 12 modes 9.30 instead of 11.96, 8 modes 6.76 instead of 7.90. The floor is optical and the same for every size: at 600 um the gap between two same-row cascade MZIs (the pitch minus their 585.5 um footprint) no longer admits the 90-degree approach of the mode net that enters the second one from the state preparation. What the sweep found that the geometry did not predict: the electrical router, not the optics, set the floor for the 12- and 16-mode diagonals (670 and 910 um) until the common bus got a straight-drop phase for lone heaters; the diagonal's one-MZI-per-column structure had no phase of the bus construction that served it, and the greedy fallback's choice flipped from a straight branch to a sideways join at the pitch where the neighbour's trunk became nearer than the stripe. The fix is one phase of the general construction, byte-identical on the suite. Lesson: a parametric sweep is a cheap way to find the router rule a fixed benchmark never exercises. Remaining: the 16-mode compact is 40 um longer than the 12 mm die; the next 10 um of pitch would cost 2.8 mm of meander, so a die fit needs a different lever (the tomography gap or the state-preparation spacing), an owner question. Queue: per-waveguide length labels in the route SVGs, the other dump circuits.


## Context and Orientation

`benchmarks/cascade.py::build_cascade_schematic(n_modes, layout)` builds the schematic (instances with placements, nets); `_column_x(column)` gives a cascade column's x from `CASCADE_COLUMN0_X_UM`, `CASCADE_COLUMN_BASE_X_UM` and `CASCADE_COLUMN_PITCH_UM`; `_column_of(mzi_index, layout)` maps MZI j to a column (folded: ceil(j/2); diagonal: j). The tomography starts `_tomography_gap_um(lane_count)` after the last cascade column's second MMI. Each benchmark module `benchmarks/cascade_<N>_<layout>.py` exposes `build_schematic()`, `NODE_TYPES`, `INTERNAL_DELAYS_UM` and the `STABLE_ROUTING_FLAGS` of `heater_s_mod` (90-degree routing, 10 um bends, path-length matching of gates and outputs, heater obstacles, electrical routing, 3 um clearance, no crossings), which `python -m photonic_router route <benchmark>` applies by default. `tests/test_cascade_benchmarks.py` checks the module counts, port usage and that the diagonal differs from the fold only in x. The flow's `SUMMARY` line (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, section 8) is the gate per run.

What limits the pitch, by the geometry: the chain connection from MZI j's lower output to MZI j+1's upper input runs straight (same y) and needs the next column's first MMI to the right of this column's second MMI (586 um plus the router's bend room); the cascade heater's pad wire rises on a corridor lane 40 um beyond the heater's electrical port (`river_routing.corridor_lane_column`), between the heater and the second MMI of its own column; the common bus trunk runs beside the heater on its bus side. Which of these binds first is what the sweep measures.
